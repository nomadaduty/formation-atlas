The Wild

Warning: wild food

Wild plants. Mississippi State University Extension: "Before eating any wild plant, you should be 100 percent certain that you have identified it correctly and that it is not poisonous."

Wild mushrooms. Poison Control: "NEVER pick and eat wild mushrooms unless they've been identified by an expert!"

If you think that someone has eaten a wild mushroom, call a poison center right away at 1-800-222-1222.

Where food comes from outside a store: what can be gathered, what takes skill, and what to do when you cannot identify something. This scene names no specific wild plant or mushroom as edible.
