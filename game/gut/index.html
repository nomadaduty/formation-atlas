What the study found

In the American Gut Project, participants who ate more than 30 different plant types per week had more diverse gut microbiomes than participants who ate 10 or fewer. This held whether participants described their diet as vegan, vegetarian, or neither. The two groups had 41 and 44 participants.

The study is observational. It shows an association. It does not show that eating more plant types causes greater microbial diversity. The study measured diversity, not health outcomes.

Sources
